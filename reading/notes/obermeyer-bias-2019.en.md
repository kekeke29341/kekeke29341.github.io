## What I took

A US care-management score used cost as a proxy for risk and assigned lower scores to Black patients at the same severity. I have not found a shorter lesson on proxy labels.

## Where I use it

When the teacher is future cost, readmission, or “a claim exists,” I write whether that is severity or access. If I cannot write it, I do not use the teacher.

## What I doubt

Japanese billing and access are not the US system. I do not import the number. I import the shape of the question.
