## What I took

A high in-hospital pneumonia detector dropped on external sites. It puts a number on the “next scanner” problem.

## Where I use it

I set this paper next to any proposal that only reports an internal split. Without another site, another scanner, or another year, the score is not an operations score.

## What I doubt

How far it drops depends on disease and device. I do not copy this one study onto every modality. I do copy the order of questions.
