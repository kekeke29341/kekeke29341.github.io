## What I took

Even with a long window, models prefer the beginning and the end and drop evidence in the middle. A wider context window is not a retrieval-quality result.

## Where I use it

Before I stuff ten chunks into a prompt, I try the top two or three and a permutation of order. If a fact that only sits in the middle disappears, the design problem is retrieval, not the window.

## What I doubt

The curve moves with model and length. I do not paste this paper’s “middle” onto every internal LLM. I borrow the question.
