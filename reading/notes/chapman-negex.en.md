## What I took

A classic rule method for negation in discharge summaries. Even with an LLM, negation versus family history versus past history is still this paper’s problem.

## Where I use it

An extractor that only reports positive-finding F1 gets a separate negation slice. I do not use “accuracy without negation.”

## What I doubt

Rules are brittle across language and note style. A neural model that never measures negation is still more dangerous than the rules.
