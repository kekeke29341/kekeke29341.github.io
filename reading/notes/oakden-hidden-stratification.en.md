## What I took

Clinically different subtypes sink under mean performance — hidden strata. A stratum that is not a label is erased by the average.

## Where I use it

I keep ids that can later cut rare findings, devices, and authors. I do not lead a hiring packet with mean AUROC I cannot cut.

## What I doubt

You cannot list every hidden stratum in advance. That is why operations has to promote failures into new strata.
