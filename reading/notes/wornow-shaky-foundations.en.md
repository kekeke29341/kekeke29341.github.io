## What I took

A review of foundation models for EHRs. The core claim: the rhetoric is large, the released weights and reproducible evals are not. Many benchmarks are billing codes or short classification, not the read/write work of a ward note.

## Where I use it

If a hospital-LLM proposal says “foundation model,” I ask for the teacher and the split first. A score that predicts ICD is not diagnostic accuracy. If patients leak across the cut, I discard the number.

## What I doubt

It is a review, not a re-run of each model. The point is not “never use these.” It is “define the measurement, then compare names.”
