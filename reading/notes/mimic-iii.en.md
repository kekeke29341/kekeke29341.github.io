## What I took

The data-descriptor for the public ICU set. Definitions of admission, stay, note, and time became the hidden premise of later benchmarks.

## Where I use it

When a paper cites MIMIC, I look for version (III / IV), a subject_id split, and how chart time versus store time is handled. If those are missing, I cannot compare.

## What I doubt

A public ICU is not a Japanese ward and not a claims file. A transfer claim has to write that gap in the body.
