## What I took

A public set of chest images plus free-text reports. It became the shared floor for vision-and-language medical papers.

## Where I use it

I check a subject split and whether serial films from one patient leak across the cut. If the report is the image’s teacher, I also check it is not exclusively written after acquisition in a way that leaks the outcome.

## What I doubt

It leans ICU in one institution. I do not carry the score onto a screening chest X-ray.
