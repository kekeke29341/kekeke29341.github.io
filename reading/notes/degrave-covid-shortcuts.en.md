## What I took

Chest-image COVID classifiers solved the laterality marker and the site style, not the lesion. I keep it as the default shortcut story for medical imaging.

## Where I use it

Before a multi-site claim, I predict site from the embedding. If that works, the disease model will use the same path. That question precedes learning-rate and augmentation talk.

## What I doubt

A saliency map that “looks at lung” does not kill the shortcut. I do not treat an explanation figure as evidence of generalization.
