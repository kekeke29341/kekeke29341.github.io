## What I took

An early operations paper: deep learning scales on a large EHR. What still matters is not the architecture. It is the time-ordered events and the definition of the prediction.

## Where I use it

“Put everything in” must be written next to “not the discharge summary, not labs after the outcome.” Serialization is preprocessing, not an explanation of performance.

## What I doubt

The numbers are one health system. Swapping in a Transformer does not retire temporal leakage.
