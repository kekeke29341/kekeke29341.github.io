## What I took

An essay: “it generalises” is a myth in clinical research and medical ML. Generalisability that does not list the shifts you survived is an empty word.

## Where I use it

I translate “it generalised” in a paper or an internal report into site, year, scanner, and note style. A sentence I cannot translate, I cut.

## What I doubt

This is not “test every shift at once.” It is “do not write an untested shift as if you tested it.”
