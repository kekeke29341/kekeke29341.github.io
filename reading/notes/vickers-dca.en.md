## What I took

Evaluate a predictor by net benefit across thresholds. I keep it next to PR on rare outcomes.

## Where I use it

I put “treat all” and “treat none” on the figure and ask whether the model wins at the threshold we would actually use. An AUROC that loses there does not page anyone.

## What I doubt

The weights are a clinical value judgment. I do not set them alone. I write the chosen weights in the caption.
