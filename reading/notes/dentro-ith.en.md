## What I took

Genetic intra-tumor heterogeneity measured across many tumors. It puts data under the limit of one biopsy.

## Where I use it

An ML paper that “predicts heterogeneity” must say whether the teacher is diversity inside the biopsy or another site. Mix those and you memorize the patient.

## What I doubt

Genetic ITH is not phenotypic or imaging heterogeneity. I do not pour modalities into one word.
