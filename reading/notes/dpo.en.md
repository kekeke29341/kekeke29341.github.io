## What I took

It replaces reward-model-plus-RL with a classification loss on preference pairs. The short implementation is why it entered operations.

## Where I use it

After SFT, I check that each pair is “which is better on the same prompt.” Two answers to different questions are not DPO. Pair construction precedes β.

## What I doubt

A short method is not a short dataset. Dirty pairs break more than SFT does.
