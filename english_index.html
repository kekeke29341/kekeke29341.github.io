About

After graduating from Keio University School of Medicine, I worked on medical × machine-learning research at the University of Tokyo. Since then I have continued implementing generative AI and ML systems, mostly through contract / embedded client work. I run a one-person company and join product and internal-adoption projects as needed.

One of my core strengths is Ruby on Rails. I have designed and shipped multiple web services — creator commerce, job boards, search, crypto-adjacent apps — from implementation through production hosting. Alongside that I work on form-PDF reading, Teams minutes on Azure, in-house generative AI, matching apps, lab-facing screens, and pricing / monitoring experiments.

I usually cover the full path from requirements through design, implementation, review, and production rollout. I try not to stop at a trial build — checking quality, permissions, operations, and cost are part of making something usable.
