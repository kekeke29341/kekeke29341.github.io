About

While studying at Keio University School of Medicine and shortly after graduating, I was selected for IPA Mitou (FY2016) and Mitou Advanced (FY2017). After graduating I did medical × machine-learning research at the University of Tokyo Graduate School of Medicine as an Excellent Young Researcher. Since then I have built ML and LLM systems through client projects, and I now run a one-person company that takes on product development and internal AI adoption under contract.

I have peer-reviewed publications at international conference workshops and in an international journal: ICML Workshop on Computational Biology (2017), NeurIPS (then called NIPS) Workshop on Machine Learning for Health (2016), NeurIPS (then called NIPS) Clinical Workshop (2015), and BioMed Research International (2017). Domestically I also presented at JAMI and JSAI. The full list is on the Papers page.

My core areas are machine learning, LLMs, and Physical AI: model selection, training and inference optimization, RAG, evaluation, and production rollout. In weekend research I also test closed-loop RGB-D perception and robot manipulation in simulation. I also handle project management, introducing AI-assisted development, and cloud / on-prem server setup.

I usually cover the full path from requirements through design, implementation, review, and production rollout. I try not to stop at a trial build — checking quality, permissions, operations, and cost are part of making something usable.

Formal numbers live in the CV; this page is a public summary. For work inquiries, please use LinkedIn or Facebook.
